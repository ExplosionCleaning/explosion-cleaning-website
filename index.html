What Our Hourly Service Includes

For both packages, the $75 hourly rate covers time spent on:

  • Consultation and planning.
  • Finding and selecting décor.
  • Shopping for approved items.
  • Arranging furniture and accessories.
  • Setup, styling, and final adjustments.

Simple, Transparent Pricing

Your total = décor purchases + decorating services at $75 per hour + approved additional expenses. For example, a $550 décor budget with four hours of service totals $850, before any applicable sales tax, delivery, or other approved expenses. You will receive an estimated budget and service hours before work begins. Purchases and spending beyond your approved budget require your approval. Décor purchased for your project is yours to keep.

Cleaning, painting, repairs, remodeling, specialized installation, and furniture rental are not included. Delivery and transportation are quoted separately when needed.
