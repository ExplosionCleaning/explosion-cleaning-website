Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 15, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out our estimate form, including your name, email address, phone number, and details about the cleaning services you are requesting.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to your estimate request, communicate with you about our services, and schedule cleaning appointments. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

We use FormSubmit.co to process form submissions and deliver them to our email inbox. We also use Google Maps to display our service area. These third-party services may collect limited technical data (such as your IP address) as described in their own privacy policies.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We do not set our own cookies for tracking or advertising purposes. However, third-party services embedded on our site — such as the Google Map in our Service Areas section — may set cookies and collect technical data (including your IP address) as described in their own privacy policies.

5. Your Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Online Privacy Protection Act (CalOPPA), California residents have the right to request to review the personal information we have collected about them and to request changes to that information. To make such a request, please contact us using the information in Section 9.

6. Do Not Track Signals

Our website does not currently respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals.

7. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed to individuals under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
